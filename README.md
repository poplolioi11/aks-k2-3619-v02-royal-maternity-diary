# 崔淑儀房護産廳日記（1694年，韓國學中央研究院藏書閣 K2-3619 第2冊） / Choe Suguibang hosan-cheong ilgi [Daily Records of the Royal Maternity Directorate for Royal Consort Choe (Sugui)]: Transcription Dataset

《崔淑儀房護産廳日記》是朝鮮護産廳1694年在漢城所編的漢文日記，記產房配置、醫官問安、醫女報告、產程與新生兒照護。底本為韓國學中央研究院藏書閣 K2-3619 冊002，起甲戌八月初一，迄九月十九。可用於研究宮廷照護程序及醫女工作。

The 1694 Choe Suguibang diary records arrangements for the delivery room, medical visits, reports from medical women, and care before and after birth in Hanseong. This is book 2 of Jangseogak shelfmark K2-3619.

依館方15幅定位，正文第003幅左至013幅左，21面，含286正文欄與一純小字格，另錄三列封面。

The 15 scans cover 21 written body faces, with 286 main-text columns and one small-writing space from scan 3 left to scan 13 left, plus three cover entries.
